How it works

FaceMesh runs entirely in this tab. It measures your eye aspect ratio — the height of the opening over its width — calibrates to your own resting value, and stops the clock on the first fast drop. No video ever leaves your device. What gets posted, if you choose to post, is a handle, a duration, and the openness curve of that one round so somebody else can race it.

Needs a webcam and a browser with WebGL. Works best with your face lit from the front.
