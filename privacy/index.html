Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026 · Dorky Robot LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Dorky Robot LLC (“Dorky Robot,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit dorkyrobot.com, use our software and services (including Kita), or communicate with us by SMS/text message or email.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us (name, email, phone number, business name).
  • Account and usage data needed to operate our products (for example login identifiers, device/browser info, and product activity logs).
  • Message content and metadata when you or your organization use our messaging features (SMS/MMS), including delivery status and opt-in/opt-out records.
  • Website analytics that may include IP address, pages viewed, and approximate location, depending on how the site is configured.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our websites, tools, and services.
  • To send transactional and service messages you request or that are part of a product you use (including appointment, reminder, and customer-care texts where your organization has configured them).
  • To respond to support requests and communicate about our products.
  • To protect security, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages from Dorky Robot or from a customer that uses our messaging platform (such as a veterinary practice using Kita), we (and our messaging providers) process your phone number and message data to deliver those texts and honor your preferences.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out; reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

We do not sell your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We share information with service providers that help us operate (for example hosting, analytics, and SMS providers such as Twilio), with your organization if you are messaging through their Kita/workspace account, when required by law, or with your direction. We do not sell personal information.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all sharing described above. This information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, accounting, and security requirements, then delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

Your choices

  • Opt out of SMS with STOP, or email us to update or delete contact data where applicable.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Email us to make a request.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
